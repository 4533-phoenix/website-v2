# 🦅 Support Phoenix 4533 Robotics

**Phoenix 4533** is a passionate high school **FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC)** team dedicated to innovation, teamwork, STEM education and Artwork both on robot and in the team areas.
Your donation helps us build competitive robots, mentor future engineers, and inspire our community.

---

## 💡 Why Donate?
- Fund robot parts, tools, and competition fees  
- Support travel expenses for regional & national events  
- Provide STEM outreach programs for younger students  
- Keep our workshop running year-round

---

## 🎯 Our Mission
We aim to empower students through hands-on engineering, programming, and leadership experiences — preparing them for bright futures in science and technology.
